## Progression
- XP to next level: `floor(100 × level^1.55)` (level 1 → 100, level 10 → 3,548).
- Kill XP: ×(1 + 0.1 × level difference), clamped to 5–150%.
- Per character level: +3 attribute points and a full restore. Per class level: +1 skill point, +1 more every 5 levels.
- Level cap: 60 (80 in NG+). Paragon levels after the cap.
- Respec: 50 gold × level. Starting gold: 50.
