## Sets

- **Golden beetles (12):** one in each of the first twelve regions. Complete: 2,400 gold and the title Beetle-Keeper.
- **God fragments (14):** one for each god, in the first fourteen regions. Complete: 4,200 gold and the title Fragment-Gatherer.
- **Crimson Pages (7):** lore pages in the first seven regions; the Hemomancer’s secret (phase 6).
- **Elder drakes (4), Saints’ shrines (8), the Clockwork Theatre and the Riddle Crown:** see the hidden-class and saint notes in PROGRESS.md.

## Secret lines

57 things people say only to heroes of a certain race, past, class or pairing. Talk to the people of the towns with each; each is said once, and pays 25 gold.

## The living world (section A spoilers)
- **Marrow** (ferry from Mirewick after asking Ferrywoman Nell): learn in one loop, then in a second run do these at once: borrow Torvik's key (forge, hour 1, 2 or 5), open the sluice (weir, hours 5 or 6), take the chapel's proof to the Mayor (chapel, hours 9 or 10), make him give the word (square, hour 11), then let Edda stop the clock (tower, hour 12). Skip the sluice and the town is only emptied. Corvin the stranger gives the order after two loops.
- **Echoes** (24): pale lore pages on the map labelled "Echo of …" in every core region; the Hollow Barrows has four that can be spoken to.
- **Festivals**: Greenwake (Springrise 7, Thornwick), The Longest Quiet (Springrise 14, Brightwater), Kitefall (Springrise 21, Lensfall), Highsun (Highsun 7, Aurelane), The Hundred Duels (Highsun 14, Sandhaven), Tidecall (Highsun 21, Saltmere), Upside Day (Highsun 28, Wreck's End), Anvilday (Leaffall 7, Khazrum), The Tally Market (Leaffall 14, Ratchet), The Deep Moot (Leaffall 21, Briarhold), The Night of Unlit Lamps (Deepcold 7, Lampfall), The Open Lectures (Deepcold 14, Frosthold), The Moonwatch (Deepcold 21, Mirewick), Turning Eve (Deepcold 28, Ashfold).

## Section D spoilers
- **Puzzle chests**: the first chest in a region needs an element in your party; the second needs a Relic Tool (grapple, glide, dowse, lantern, phase), found by deeds: 8 dungeons, 10 zones, 12 secrets, 20 lore pages, 10 bosses.
- **Observatory** (Frosthold): gather six clues and answer the seven dials from the Turning Wheel, the Marrow loop and the Grudgebound; see `data/realm/observatory.ts` for the answers.
- **Emberfall**: Ashfold, settle for 60; raids grow with the scars of your foes, so build the watchtower early.
- **Celestial Atlas**: the tenth map of each constellation gives 3 stars; the Weeping Star (last constellation) is the hardest and pays 40% bounty.

- **Nymira's Waters**: each legend rises only in its own water at its hour (see `LEGENDS` in `data/wonders.ts`) once you have caught enough kinds; a rarity 4-5 fish that escapes comes back angrier and pays more when landed.
- **Spire of Cards**: relics for a sphere add +2 power to that sphere's cards; The Gambler's Grace makes draws wins.
- **Painter's Eye commissions**: Dawn Over the Meadow (Meadowlands, day, clear), Lights on the Fen (Moonfen, night), Storm Coast in a storm, Frostspire in snow, Ashen Desert in a sandstorm, Volcanic Rift at night, Whisperwood in fog, any town in rain, a photo with a pet, Merrowdeep, Celestial Realm by day, Hollow Barrows at night.

## Section E spoilers
