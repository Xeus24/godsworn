# Music credits

The optional "Composed (open licence)" music in Godsworn is human-made. All six
tracks are by **Juhani Junkala** (https://juhanijunkala.com/), released under
**CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/),
and were published on OpenGameArt by SubspaceAudio. No attribution is
required; it is given here as thanks.
